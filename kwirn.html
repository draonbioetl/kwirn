Dot, then line

On your turn, place one dot anywhere, then join any two of your dots with a straight line.

Cross to score

Score 1 point for each opponent line your new line crosses. You can never cross your own lines.

Loops are armour

Lines that form any closed loop are drawn thick and can't be crossed.

Cast a net

Closing a loop removes every dot inside it, with their lines. No one may place a dot inside a closed shape. Points already scored stay.

Wet ink

A line drawn on your opponent's latest turn is still wet (dotted) and can't be crossed yet. It dries once you've moved.

First to 20

The first player to reach the target wins. If no line is possible, you just place your dot.

Controls
Click the paper to place a dot, then drag between two of your dots (or click both).
Or aim first: drag out from one of your dots and release where the new dot should go. The preview shows what the line will score.
Click empty paper again to move your new dot · Esc take it back
